Try the whole loop

  1. Home is Mia's portfolio: total value, the chart, holdings (tap one for its details), her milestone and learning. Invest is where Sam acts: market status, wallet, three ways to invest, and the list of ASX-listed ETFs.
  2. Invest now: open Invest, tap an ETF, enter an amount and press Buy now. The market is open, so it is bought straight away.
  3. Set Jump to at the top to Weekend, closed. Buy again: the order waits with a live countdown to Monday 10:00 am. Cancel it, or press Skip to market open to see it filled.
  4. Steady plan: choose how much PayTo collects, how often, and the amount (at least $100) at which it auto-invests into your chosen ETFs.
  5. Milestones: in Approvals, approve Mia's car wash and shout-out. She passes 200 credits. Open Invest → Milestones, add money (the wallet only has $90 free) and approve. You can also change the milestone amount for each level (at least $100).
  6. Press Next month to move time on. Prices move, the steady plan collects money, and Mia's garden grows.
  7. Turn on Show review notes to tag everything a lawyer should check.

What one month does

The clock jumps to a weekday morning with the market open, so waiting orders are filled. Made-up prices move, the steady plan collects money into the wallet (and auto-invests once your amount is reached), repeating tasks reopen, and the monthly cap and shout-out limit reset.

Market hours in the demo: about 10 am to 4 pm Sydney time on weekdays. Public holidays are ignored. In the live app the partner says when the market is open.

The ETF list is a made-up catalogue of 31 example funds in 10 categories. In the live app it would be the partner's full list of ASX (Australian Securities Exchange) ETFs (exchange-traded funds).

Credits are points. No real money, broker, login or fund is connected. Funds, fees and returns are made up. Not predictions or advice.
